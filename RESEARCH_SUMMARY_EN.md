# Taiwan Vendor Brand Research Results

**Purpose**: Prepare a vendor list for Taiwan companies exhibiting abroad, confirming which vendors have their own brands

**Survey Date**: 2026-01-14

## Executive Summary

This research analyzed 182 Taiwanese companies to determine their brand status for international exhibition participation.

### Key Statistics
- **Total Companies**: 182
- **Own Brand Manufacturers**: ~120 companies (suitable for international exhibitions)
- **OEM/ODM Focused**: ~49 companies (conditional invitation)
- **Recommended for Removal**: 13 entities (associations, research institutes, media companies)

## Classification Distribution

### Own Brand Companies (~120)
These companies have established their own brands and are suitable for international exhibitions:
- Semiconductors & IC: MediaTek, Himax, Etron, Sunplus
- Power & Electrical: Delta, TECO, Shihlin, Fortune
- Test Equipment: GW Instek, TENMARS, TES, Lutron, Brymen
- Communication: WNC, GlobalSat
- Components: Taiwan Alpha, Y.S. TECH, SNOWFAN
- And many more specialized brands...

### OEM/ODM Companies (~49)
These companies primarily focus on contract manufacturing:
- May consider invitation if they plan to develop own brands
- Good manufacturing capabilities but limited brand recognition
- Examples: Various connector, cable, and component manufacturers

### Non-Manufacturers (13)
Should be removed from exhibition list:
- Industry associations (TEEMA, TCA, TDUA)
- Research institutes (ITRI, III)
- Academic institutions
- Media companies
- Government agencies

## Recommendations

### Priority 1: Top-Tier Brands (9 companies)
Invite first - these are Taiwan's representative brands with international recognition

### Priority 2: Second-Tier Brands (~111 companies)
Strong invitation - these companies have own brands suitable for international exhibitions

### Priority 3: OEM/ODM Companies (~49 companies)
Conditional invitation - consider if they have plans to develop own brands

### Do Not Invite: Non-Manufacturers (13 entities)
Remove from list - associations, institutions, media, etc.

## Notes

1. Research based on publicly available information and web searches
2. Some companies may have both own brand and OEM/ODM operations
3. Recommend confirming participation willingness before formal invitation
4. Companies marked as "agent" or "trader" may be considered if representing quality brands
