# Taiwan Vendor Brand Research Results

**Purpose**: Prepare a vendor list for Taiwan companies exhibiting abroad, confirming which vendors have their own Taiwan brands

**Survey Date**: 2026-01-14

**Update**: Adjusted criteria - Non-Taiwan brands and companies without own brands are now recommended for removal

## Executive Summary

This research analyzed 182 Taiwanese companies to determine their brand status for international exhibition participation. **Updated filtering criteria** now exclude companies without own brands and those representing foreign brands.

### Key Statistics
- **Total Companies**: 182
- **Recommended to Keep (Taiwan Brands)**: 115 companies (63.2%)
  - Tier 1 Global Brands: 9 companies
  - Tier 2 Notable Taiwan Brands: 13 companies
  - Other Taiwan Brands: 93 companies
- **Recommended for Removal**: 67 companies (36.8%)
  - Non-manufacturers (associations, institutes, media): 13 companies
  - No own brand (OEM/ODM only): 44 companies
  - Non-Taiwan brands: 4 companies
  - Duplicates: 6 companies

## Update Notes

**January 14, 2026 Update**: Adjusted filtering criteria based on new requirements:

1. **Companies without own brands** → Recommended for removal (44 companies)
   - OEM/ODM manufacturers without brands
   - Trading companies and agents
   - Service providers without products

2. **Non-Taiwan brands** → Recommended for removal (4 companies)
   - Japanese brands: Anritsu, Sankosha
   - Irish brand: Taoglas
   - Canadian brand: EDAC

These adjustments ensure the exhibition list focuses on **Taiwan manufacturers with Taiwan-owned brands**, better aligning with the goal of promoting Taiwan brands internationally.
